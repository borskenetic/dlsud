ABOUT DLSU-D

De La Salle University – Dasmariñas (DLSU-D) is a Lasallian university in Brgy. Fatima 1, Dasmariñas City, Cavite, Philippines 4115. The university seal carries the motto Faith, Service, and Communion. Its public site is www.dlsud.edu.ph.

This portal is the university library and attendance system. Students and staff can search the catalog, register for library access, record attendance, and request a room.
